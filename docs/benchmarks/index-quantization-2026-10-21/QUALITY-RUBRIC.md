# Source-based quality rubric

Established before comparative review. Review every unique translation, including repeat differences. Labels are shuffled independently for each source; the key is kept separate until annotations are written. Reviewer has seen two baseline smoke outputs (date/condition); those cases are not fully blind. A single assistant's qualitative review is exploratory, not an independent expert or native-speaker panel.

- **Major meaning error:** wrong negation/condition, changed important number/date/entity/actor, omitted proposition, invented substantive fact, wrong target language, or truncated/failed output.
- **Minor issue:** localized grammatical/terminological awkwardness with recoverable meaning, small specificity loss, or formatting defect without changed core facts.
- **Acceptable variation:** synonymous wording, idiomatic reordering, conventional name transliteration, or an interpretation genuinely permitted by an ambiguous source.

Assess source fidelity, not agreement with Q4_K_M or a single reference. For isolated “bank” phrases, either valid contextual interpretation is allowed; context-rich counterparts must follow the given meaning. Flag multiple issues separately but count affected cases once at each severity. Publish concrete spans and explanations, both improvements and regressions; no general claim from 30 RU/EN cases to all supported languages.
