# iOS website demonstration — 23 October 2026

The owner chose iPhone Simulator capture instead of a connected-phone recording. The local website now includes a 38.5-second, silent 1280 × 720 / 30 fps H.264 montage, animated Kat logo and pawprints, slide/dissolve transitions, poster, download link and EN/RU/ES/TR WebVTT subtitles. Playback is user initiated, with no video preload. English titles are embedded; the page selects translated subtitles for other languages.

## Actual app footage

Ordinary simulator app, iPhone 17 Pro profile, iOS 27.0, development 1.1.0 (73); Index 2B CPU, English interface, EN → RU, automatic speech off. Existing model files were reused. The example was typed and translated by the app:

- Input: “Two coffees and a window seat, please.”
- Output: “Два кофе и место у окна, пожалуйста.”

The sequence shows text entry, model preparation/translation, the actual result, Settings → Models and voices → Voices. Captured pixels are scaled into a phone surround without replacement. This is not the prepared-state screenshot helper. Launch/preparation before the selected scenes is omitted; retained scenes play at 1× with 0.4-second transitions. No microphone or physical-device speed claim. Native Mac Kat remained closed during simulator use; the simulator was shut down afterwards. No installed physical app or model was replaced.

## Capture and validation boundaries

Two capture attempts were made. The first readiness condition was too early; it did not establish completed translation. The second recording contains the completed output and all selected navigation scenes. The XCTest run nevertheless ended with a failure at the final close of a nested Settings view (missing close identifier), after the required scenes. It is not reported as a passing UI test. That failed tail is excluded.

Source timestamps were visually checked. Variable-frame-rate simulator footage was normalized to constant frame rate before seeking; otherwise seeking inside held frames selected the wrong scene. The corrected result frame was visually checked against the source. Final video decode and local browser playback/caption selection are checked separately from capture automation.
